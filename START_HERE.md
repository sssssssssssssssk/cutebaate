## 🎓 Understanding Your App

### **How It Works:**
1. **User creates a session** → Gets random Session ID & Password
2. **Other user joins** → Enters ID & Password
3. **Connection established** → P2P WebRTC link
4. **Messages encrypted** → AES-256 encryption
5. **Messages sent directly** → Peer-to-peer, not through servers
6. **No storage** → Messages deleted when session ends
7. **Fully anonymous** → No data collection

### **Why It's Secure:**
- ✅ End-to-end encryption (AES-256)
- ✅ Peer-to-peer connections (no middle server)
- ✅ Zero message storage
- ✅ No registration needed
- ✅ No personal data collected
- ✅ Military-grade encryption

### **How You Make Money:**
- 💰 Google AdSense ads on your website
- 💰 Ads serve automatically
- 💰 You earn per impression & click
- 💰 Estimated: $2-5 per 1,000 visitors
- 💰 Minimum payout: $100 (usually in 30-60 days)
